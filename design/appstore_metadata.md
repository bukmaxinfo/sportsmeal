## Description
SportsMeal uses Claude AI to analyze your meal photos and instantly estimate calories, protein, carbs, and fat — no manual logging required.

**Snap a Photo, Get the Numbers**
Simply photograph your meal and SportsMeal identifies each food item with calorie and macro breakdowns. It understands portion sizes, cooking methods, and cuisines from Chinese to Mediterranean.

**Smart Tracking That Works for You**
• Calorie ring shows your daily progress at a glance
• Macro rings track protein, carbs, and fat against personalized targets
• Save favorite meals as templates for one-tap quick logging
• Streak tracking keeps you motivated day after day

**Beyond Just Calories**
• Scan barcodes on packaged foods for instant nutrition data
• Photograph restaurant menus to find dishes that fit your budget
• AI meal suggestions based on your remaining calories and diet
• Smart pantry management with expiration tracking
• Recipe generation from your available ingredients

**Built for Your Body**
• Personalized calorie budgets based on your BMR and activity level
• Support for 8 diet types: Keto, Paleo, Vegan, Mediterranean, and more
• Exercise tracking with MET-based calorie burn calculations
• Weight loss projections based on safe, sustainable rates

**Apple Health Integration**
Sync weight, steps, and active calories with Apple Health. Your workout sessions and meal data flow seamlessly between apps.

**Privacy First**
Your API key is stored securely in the iOS Keychain. Meal photos are analyzed via the Anthropic API and never stored on external servers. All personal data stays on your device.

Available in English and Chinese (Simplified).
